Day-wise on-hand

Opening and closing quantity each day, what was sold, and how much was sold without stock.

How this is worked out: Odoo doesn't keep stock levels per day, so each day is rebuilt from today's on-hand quantity by walking back through every completed stock move. Sold without stock counts the units of each sale that were more than what was on hand at that moment. Days follow your computer's time zone.
